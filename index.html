Help Center

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How long does shipping take?▾
Orders are packed within 24 to 48 hours. Metro deliveries arrive in 2–4 business days; the rest of India takes 4–7 business days.
2. How do I pay via UPI without gateway errors?▾
Select Instant UPI at checkout. A live Dynamic QR Code with your exact bill appears, or click the direct GPay/PhonePe button. Enter your 12-digit UTR reference number from your bank receipt to log the order.
3. What is your return & exchange policy?▾
We provide a 5-day replacement policy for defective, damaged, or incorrect size garments upon parcel arrival.

Store Terms & Policies

Shipping Policy

All items ship through BlueDart, Delhivery, or DTDC. Complimentary shipping is automatically applied on all orders above ₹999. Standard flat ₹79 delivery fee applies on orders below ₹999.

5-Day Return & Replacement Policy

To initiate a size exchange or report transit damage, email zayracarts@gmail.com with unboxing media within 5 calendar days of package receipt. Items must be unworn with original tags attached.

Privacy & Data Safety

We respect customer privacy. Your delivery addresses, phone numbers, and transactional records are encrypted and never sold or shared with external advertising brokers.
