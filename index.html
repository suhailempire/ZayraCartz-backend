Frequently Asked Questions

1. How long does delivery take?

Dispatched within 24-48 hours. Metro cities arrive in 2–4 business days; rest of India in 4–7 business days.

2. How does the UPI payment work?

You scan our dynamic QR code or open GPay/PhonePe directly. Enter your 12-digit UTR reference number from your bank app, and we verify the credit before dispatch.

3. What is your return & exchange window?

We offer a 5-day replacement policy for damaged, defective, or incorrect size garments upon delivery.

Shipping Policy

All ZayraCartz orders are shipped via premier courier partners (BlueDart, Delhivery, and DTDC).

Shipping Charges: Free express shipping on all prepaid and COD orders above ₹999. Standard ₹79 flat delivery fee on orders below ₹999.

Tracking AWB numbers are updated in your order lookup dashboard as soon as the package leaves our warehouse.

Returns & Refund Policy

If you receive a defective or incorrect size item, raise an exchange request within 5 days of parcel arrival by emailing zayracarts@gmail.com with unboxing photos.

Garments must be unworn, unwashed, and in original packaging with tags intact. Replacement will be dispatched upon reverse inspection.
