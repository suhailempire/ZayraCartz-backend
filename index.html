Help Center

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How long does shipping take?▾
Orders are dispatched within 24 to 48 hours. Metro deliveries arrive in 2–4 business days; the rest of India takes 4–7 business days.
2. How do I pay via UPI without gateway errors?▾
Select Instant UPI at checkout. A live Dynamic QR Code with your exact bill appears, or click the direct GPay/PhonePe button. Enter your 12-digit UTR reference number from your bank receipt to log the order.
3. What is your return & exchange policy?▾
We provide a 5-day replacement policy for defective, damaged, or incorrect items upon parcel arrival.

Shipping Policy

All items ship through BlueDart, Delhivery, or DTDC. Complimentary express delivery is automatically applied on all orders above ₹999. Standard flat ₹79 delivery fee applies on orders below ₹999.

5-Day Return & Replacement Policy

To initiate a size exchange or report transit damage, email zayracarts@gmail.com with unboxing media within 5 calendar days of package receipt. Items must be in original condition with tags intact.
